# Tier 5 · Expert & Modern Systems (`18` – `26`)

Master the highest echelon of modern C engineering: cache-conscious data structures, ISO C23 (`ISO/IEC 9899:2024`), cross-compilation, hardware PMU profiling, offensive/defensive exploit mitigations, lock-free memory reclamation, multi-language FFI, Unicode, and automated fuzzing.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

- **18 · Advanced Data Structures**

    Balanced trees (AVL, Red-Black), Robin-Hood & chained hash tables, binary heaps, skip lists, prefix tries, and generic C23 containers.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Open Module 18](18-advanced-data-structures.md)

- **19 · Modern C (C99, C11, C17, C23 & C2y)**

    Full tour of modern ISO C: `nullptr`, `constexpr`, `typeof`, `auto`, `#embed`, `[[attributes]]`, `<stdbit.h>`, `<stdckdint.h>`, and C2y roadmap.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Open Module 19](19-modern-c-c99-to-c23.md)

- **20 · CMake & Cross-Compilation**

    Modern target-based CMake, CTest integration, cross-compilation toolchain files, and automated static analysis gates.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Open Module 20](20-cmake-cross-compilation.md)

- **21 · Debugging, Valgrind & `perf`**

    GDB hardware watchpoints, Valgrind Memcheck/Massif, Linux PMU `perf` counters, Flame Graphs, `strace`/`ltrace`, and ELF tools.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Open Module 21](21-debugging-profiling-perf.md)

- **22 · Security Hardening & Exploit Mitigations**

    Stack/heap/format/integer exploits, ROP, ASLR/PIE/NX/RELRO/CFI/PAC, C23 `<stdckdint.h>` & `memset_explicit`, SEI CERT C & MISRA-C.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Open Module 22](22-security-exploits-mitigations.md)

- **23 · Lock-Free, Hazard Pointers & RCU**

    Progress guarantees, ABA defense, MPSC/MPMC lock-free queues, hazard pointers, epoch-based reclamation, and Read-Copy-Update (RCU).

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Open Module 23](23-lock-free-hazard-pointers-rcu.md)

- **24 · ABI, FFI, `dlopen` & Linker Scripts**

    System V & AAPCS64 ABIs, `extern "C"`, `dlopen`/`dlsym`, `LD_PRELOAD`, symbol version scripts, GNU `ld` linker scripts, Python/Rust/Go/Java FFI.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Open Module 24](24-ffi-dynamic-loading-linker-scripts.md)

- **25 · Unicode, Locales & Complex Math**

    ASCII/UTF-8/16/32, C23 `char8_t`, `<wchar.h>`, `<uchar.h>`, `<locale.h>`, `<complex.h>`, and C29 `<stdmchar.h>`.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Open Module 25](25-unicode-locale-complex-math.md)

- **26 · Testing, Fuzzing & Software Quality**

    Unit testing (Unity, cmocka), `--wrap` dependency injection, `gcov` coverage, libFuzzer/AFL++, and CI verification pipelines.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Open Module 26](26-testing-fuzzing-quality.md)

</div>
